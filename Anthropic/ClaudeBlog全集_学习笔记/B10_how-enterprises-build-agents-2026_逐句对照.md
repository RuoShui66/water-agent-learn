AI agents have moved from experimental to an essential part of an organization's tech stack.

AI 智能体已经从实验品变成了组织技术栈的必备部分。

But how are enterprises actually using them?

但企业实际上在怎么用它们？

In partnership with research firm Material, we surveyed over 500 technical leaders across industries and company sizes to understand how organizations are deploying agents today and where they see opportunity ahead.

我们与调研公司 Material 合作，调查了跨行业、跨公司规模的 500 多位技术负责人，以了解组织今天如何部署智能体、以及他们看到的未来机会在哪。

The findings show a clear pattern: organizations are shifting from simple task automation to complex, multi-step workflows that span teams and business functions.

调研结果显示出清晰的模式：组织正在从简单的任务自动化，转向横跨团队和业务职能的复杂多步工作流。

## What the data shows

## 数据说明了什么

More than half of organizations (57%) now deploy agents for multi-stage workflows, with 16% running cross-functional processes across multiple teams.

超过半数的组织（57%）已在多阶段工作流中部署智能体，16% 在多个团队之间运行跨职能流程。

In 2026, 81% plan to tackle more complex use cases, including 39% developing agents for multi-step processes and 29% deploying them for cross-functional projects.

2026 年，81% 计划挑战更复杂的用例，其中 39% 在开发多步流程智能体，29% 计划将智能体部署到跨职能项目。

Coding leads adoption.

编码引领采纳。

Nearly 90% of organizations use AI to assist with development, and 86% deploy agents for production code.

近 90% 的组织用 AI 辅助开发，86% 在生产代码中部署智能体。

Organizations report time savings across the entire development lifecycle: planning and ideation (58%), code generation (59%), documentation (59%), and code review and testing (59%).

组织报告在整个开发生命周期都省了时间：规划与构思（58%）、代码生成（59%）、文档（59%）、代码评审与测试（59%）。

But the impact extends beyond engineering.

但影响不止于工程。

Data analysis and report generation (60%) and internal process automation (48%) rank among the highest-impact use cases.

数据分析与报告生成（60%）、内部流程自动化（48%）位列影响最大的用例。

Looking ahead, 56% plan to implement agents for research and reporting over the next year.

展望未来，56% 计划在明年为研究和报告场景引入智能体。

Perhaps most notably, 80% of organizations report their AI agent investments are already delivering measurable economic returns.

或许最值得注意的是：80% 的组织报告其 AI 智能体投资已经带来可度量的经济回报。

## What this looks like in practice

## 实践中长什么样

The organizations seeing results are treating agents as a core part of their infrastructure, not experiments.

见到成效的组织，都是把智能体当作基础设施的核心部分，而不是实验。

Thomson Reuters uses Claude to power CoCounsel, their AI legal platform.

Thomson Reuters 用 Claude 驱动其 AI 法律平台 CoCounsel。

Lawyers who once spent hours manually searching through documents can now access 150 years of case law and 3,000 domain experts in minutes.

过去要花数小时手动翻文档的律师，现在几分钟内就能访问 150 年的判例法和 3000 位领域专家（的知识）。

Cybersecurity company eSentire compressed expert threat analysis from 5 hours to 7 minutes, with AI-driven analysis aligning with their senior security experts 95% of the time.

网络安全公司 eSentire 把专家级威胁分析从 5 小时压缩到 7 分钟，AI 驱动的分析与其资深安全专家的一致率达 95%。

In healthcare, Doctolib rolled out Claude Code across their entire engineering team, replacing legacy testing infrastructure in hours instead of weeks and shipping features 40% faster.

医疗领域，Doctolib 在整个工程团队推广 Claude Code，把替换遗留测试基础设施的时间从数周缩短到数小时，功能发布提速 40%。

The retail sector is seeing similar gains.

零售行业也在看到类似收益。

L'Oréal achieved 99.9% accuracy on conversational analytics, enabling 44,000 monthly users to query data directly instead of waiting for custom dashboards.

欧莱雅在对话式分析上达到 99.9% 准确率，让 4.4 万月活用户可以直接查询数据，而不用等定制仪表盘。

## The path forward

## 前路

The question for leaders in 2026 isn't whether to adopt AI agents but how to scale them strategically.

2026 年摆在管理者面前的问题，不是要不要采纳 AI 智能体，而是如何有策略地扩展它们。

The data points to three primary challenges: integration with existing systems (46%), data access and quality (42%), and change management needs (39%).

数据指向三大挑战：与现有系统的集成（46%）、数据访问与质量（42%）、变革管理需求（39%）。

Nine in 10 leaders report that agents are shifting how their teams work, with employees spending more time on strategic activities, relationship building, and skill development rather than routine execution.

十分之九的负责人报告智能体正在改变团队的工作方式：员工把更多时间花在战略性活动、关系建设和技能发展上，而不是例行执行。

This transition requires purpose-built infrastructure: models optimized for coding and enterprise workflows, frameworks like the Agent SDK, and tools like Claude Code that help teams move from prototype to production faster.

这个转型需要专门构建的基础设施：为编码和企业工作流优化的模型、Agent SDK 这样的框架、以及 Claude Code 这样帮团队更快从原型走向生产的工具。

We're also finding that while coding has been the proving ground for AI agents, it's just the beginning.

我们还发现：编码固然是 AI 智能体的试验场，但这只是开始。

As agents expand into research, customer service, financial planning, and supply chain operations, the organizations that build expertise now will capture disproportionate value as the technology matures.

随着智能体扩展到研究、客服、财务规划和供应链运营，现在就积累专长的组织，将在技术成熟时收获不成比例的价值。
